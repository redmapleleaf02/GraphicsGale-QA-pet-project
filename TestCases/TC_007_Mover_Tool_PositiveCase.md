# GraphicsGale - Test Case 7

## ID: TC007
## Title: Using the "Mover" tool. Positive case

### Preconditions:
- User has an installed application;
- Application is accessible;
- User has an opened canvas in the workspace.

### Steps:
1. Click on the "Zoom"/"Magnifier" tool;
2. Left-click on the canvas to zoom in until the right and bottom scrollbars appear;
3. Click on the "Mover" tool;
4. Click and drag to move the visible area of the canvas when the canvas is larger than the window.

### Expected Result:
- The tool moves the visible area of the canvas.
